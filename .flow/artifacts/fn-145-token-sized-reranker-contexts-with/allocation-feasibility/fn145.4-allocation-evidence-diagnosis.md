## Precise missing datum and a conditional smallest control

To demonstrate the missing Metal R3 claim, the minimum allocation evidence is a **matched physical measurement in bytes**, with the same real model/backend/layer placement/formatted short input, at equivalent loaded-model, context-created, after-first-score and after-warm-score boundaries for auto and sized contexts. It must identify whether the measured quantity is native context state, Metal allocated buffers, device/unified used memory, or whole process footprint; those quantities are not interchangeable. Complete scoring and creation/cold/warm timings are separately necessary to finish the rest of R3.

The installed node-llama-cpp3.19.1 declarations expose:

- LlamaContext.stateSize: actual llama.cpp state bytes, explicitly **excluding some context overhead** (`dist/evaluator/LlamaContext/LlamaContext.d.ts:36`).
- Llama.getVramState: total used/free/unified device memory; it is not a per-context counter (`dist/bindings/Llama.d.ts:69`).
- GgufInsights.estimateContextResourceRequirementsV2: an **estimate**, suitable as one admission input, not observed allocation proof.

None of those values was retained by the old Metal helpers. StateSize alone could support a specifically named state-byte reduction, but would not prove total native allocation savings. Device/unified snapshots require stable background activity and explicit attribution limits; direct native buffer logs/counters would be stronger.

**No new safe launch is justified from the old receipts alone.** Prior pressure stops occurred even in the reranker-only helper, with substantial existing swap use. Final child cleanup improves ownership/containment; it does not make auto40960 consume fewer bytes. The exact reason pressure changed cannot be narrowed to “old lifecycle bug” from these samples.

If the host elects a new independent experiment, the smallest plausible design is two fresh, sequential final guarded native-child lifetimes, each loading only the same reranker and scoring the same complete short prepared pairs once cold and once warm. One uses final sizing, the other a development-only explicit native-auto control at the context factory. This changes only context-sizing policy; it must not alter templates, tokenization, weights, precision, candidate count, documents or query. It is a new controlled-final-runtime comparison, not a relabelled270c baseline. Every context must be disposed and the child fully gone before the other allocation begins; no simultaneous auto+sized contexts or hidden warmup.

Admission must first establish new credible fit evidence: a stable normal-pressure window, actual free/unified-memory/footprint observations, conservative full model+auto-context+scoring-scratch estimate with reserve, and absence of prior owned allocations. If auto selects a different capacity under new available-memory conditions, record it; do not force or rename it40960. Do not disable the library memory checks, change GPU layers/precision/batch policy to squeeze the control in, relax the pressure guard, or terminate unrelated user workloads. A pressure stop remains an incomplete row with its raw evidence; no retry-to-green.

Without those changed, measured safe conditions, retain R3 Metal as bounded/incomplete. A further unsafe oldauto attempt is not required simply to fill the acceptance table.
